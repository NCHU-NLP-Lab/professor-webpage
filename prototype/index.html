A Profile

Yao-Chung Fan 范耀中

Vice Provost · Full Professor of Computer Science · Director, NCHU Natural Language Processing Laboratory

A computer scientist at National Chung Hsing University, where he leads the NLP Laboratory and serves as Vice Provost of Academic Affairs. His work moves between basic research in language generation and the deployment of agentic AI systems serving students, hospital partners, and Indigenous-language communities.

I. Three Tracks

In a typical week he attends to three concurrent obligations. As Vice Provost, he participates in the governance of NCHU's Office of Academic Affairs. As a full professor, he teaches and publishes on natural language generation, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic AI. As director of the NCHU NLP Laboratory, he oversees the deployment of production systems used across campus and beyond.

The arrangement is deliberate. Research without deployment risks irrelevance; deployment without research risks superficiality; neither survives long without institutional standing.

II. The Lab's Work, in Production

The NCHU NLP Laboratory operates a family of deployed systems — not prototypes but live services running for actual users on and beyond campus.

  1. AI 興學伴 Live

    A campus-wide learning advisor that integrates NCHU's institutional information — course search, faculty lookup, learning resources — into a single conversational interface. Positioned as the university's LearningHub.

    Audience: NCHU students & faculty LLM · RAG · Campus integration

    eduxplore.nlpnchu.org

  2. 神農 TAIDE Live

    A retrieval-augmented generation system built on top of TAIDE, the Taiwan-developed traditional-Chinese large language model.

    TAIDE · RAG

  3. Fact-Checking System Live

    An end-to-end fact verification architecture using Anthropic Claude and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to coordinate retrieval, evidence checking, and verdict generation.

    Claude API · MCP · Agent

  4. Changhua Christian Hospital Consultation Live

    A smart question-answering system for hospital visiting rules and policies, deployed in partnership with Changhua Christian Hospital. The laboratory's first major cross-institution deployment, demonstrating that research-to-production can extend beyond university boundaries.

    Healthcare RAG Cross-institution

  5. Amis (阿美族語) Dialogue System Research / Deployed

    A combined large language model and speech-recognition system for Amis, a low-resource Indigenous Taiwanese language. The system supports preservation, education, and accessibility for Amis-speaking communities.

    LLM · ASR · Indigenous Language · Low-resource
